He was told to wait.
He chose to lead instead.

“Fight. Recover. Because you can. And live life to the fullest.”— Louis Gibson

Louis Gibson came home from active duty with a severe traumatic brain injury and a spinal-cord injury, then spent years navigating poor care and false narratives before finding the providers who helped him rebuild. That road became his mission: no one who served should face recovery, a legal fight, or a hard night by themselves.

In 2018 he founded Legal Protection Agent to connect people failed by broken systems with qualified legal help. As LiveHero’s U.S. Ambassador he carries the same promise to veterans and their families across the country, his way: helping through teaching, not telling.

    Who we are

    To restore and save
    the life and livelihoods.

    Live Hero, Inc. is a national veterans’ service organization. Our mission is to alleviate suffering and to enhance the life and living of those who have served in defense of national security, and to support the veterans injured while defending our freedoms.

    01

    Comradeship

    Foster comradeship among veterans, so no one who served walks the road home alone.

    02

    Honor the fallen

    Honor the memory of deceased veterans and comfort the families they left behind.

    03

    Patriotism

    Encourage patriotism and the spirit of service in our communities and the next generation.

    04

    Aid in need

    Aid hospitalized, disabled, and needy veterans and their dependents with care, shelter, food, health, education, and financial help.

    “Whoever saves a single life is considered to have saved the whole world.”Jewish proverb · the line LiveHero was founded on

    Live Hero, Inc. also works for the social welfare of veterans and their dependents, widows, and orphans; provides entertainment and care for hospitalized veterans; sponsors memorial, charitable, educational, and patriotic programs; and offers social and recreational activities for members.

    Good to know

    Giving, answered.

    Is my donation tax-deductible?

    Yes. LiveHero is a registered 501(c)(19) veterans organization. Gifts are tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law, and you'll receive an emailed receipt immediately.

    Can I change or cancel a monthly gift?

    Any time. Email us or use the link in your receipt to update the amount, payment method, or frequency.

    How is my gift used?

    The large majority of every dollar goes directly to veteran programs: crisis support, housing, career transition, and family services. We publish our financials annually.

    Can I give by check or wire?

    Absolutely. Make checks payable to LiveHero and mail them to our office, or contact us for wire instructions and securities transfer details.

    Will you share my information?

    Never. We do not sell, rent, or trade donor information.
